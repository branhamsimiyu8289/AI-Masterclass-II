AI AGENT WORKFLOW PROPOSAL
=========================

## Opening

Your team can spend less time on repetitive work and more time serving
customers by using AI agents to streamline a key business process. I can help
you select a practical starting point, then design and implement a workflow
that works with your existing tools and keeps your team in control.

## Understanding

To shape a solution around your needs, we’ll first agree on the process or
department to automate and the outcomes that matter most. I’ll learn how the
work is handled today, where delays or manual bottlenecks occur, and which
tasks should remain with staff. We’ll identify required tools, access,
exception handling, and launch timing. This discovery ensures the scope is
clear before development begins.

## Approach

I’ll map the agreed workflow and identify suitable tasks for AI assistance.
Next, I’ll configure the agents, connect them to the selected tools, and test
the workflow using representative scenarios. Together, we’ll test routine
cases and confirm when it should flag decisions for human review. I’ll make
agreed adjustments before
handover, with a focus on reliability, usability, and a clear path for your
team to manage the process.

## Deliverables

You’ll receive a working AI-agent workflow integrated with the agreed tools,
documentation covering its operation and key review points, and a 30-minute
team handover.

## Investment and timeline

The fixed project price is KES 45,000, with 50% payable upfront and 50% upon
delivery. Estimated delivery is 10 working days after I receive the required
system access and we confirm the workflow requirements.

## Pricing assumption

The project price assumes your existing systems, data, and licenses are
available for the workflow. Any new third-party subscriptions, API usage, or
hosting fees are not included and will be discussed and approved separately
before they are incurred.
