# Project Agreement: Automated School Attendance SMS Script

## Project overview

This agreement outlines the scope, timeline, and payment terms for developing
a custom Python script to automate student attendance SMS notifications for
the school.

## 1. Scope of work

### Included

- Develop a Python script that reads student attendance data from an Excel
  file.
- Identify students who have been absent for three or more days in a given
  week.
- Integrate with an SMS gateway API to send automated text messages to
  parents.

### Not included

- Ongoing maintenance, hosting, or server management after final delivery and
  handover.
- SMS gateway subscription credits or airtime bundles. The school will
  maintain its own SMS account.
- Data entry or manual cleaning of the school's historical Excel attendance
  records.

## 2. Payment terms

The total project budget is **KES 22,000**.

- **Deposit (50%): KES 11,000**, due before work begins.
- **Final payment (50%): KES 11,000**, due within two business days of
  successful delivery and confirmation of project testing.

Work will begin once the deposit has cleared.

## 3. Timeline

The project will start once the 50% deposit has cleared. Delivery is estimated
within **seven working days** of the official start date.

## 4. Out-of-scope requests

Requests for additional features, modifications, or support beyond the agreed
scope after project delivery will be billed separately at an hourly or
milestone rate agreed upon by both parties.
