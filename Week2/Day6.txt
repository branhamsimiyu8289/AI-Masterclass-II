CLIENT BRIEF
============

## Project Summary

The client is looking for a freelancer to build and implement AI agents for
their business. The project includes designing agent workflows, integrating
tools, and ensuring the system runs reliably. They are looking for someone who
can work independently, communicate clearly, and deliver practical solutions
that improve existing processes.

## Brief Analysis

**The problem**

The business may have manual bottlenecks or inefficient processes, but it
doesn’t have the in-house technical resources to design, build, and maintain
automated AI-agent workflows.

**The deliverable**

A set of functional, integrated AI agents with defined workflows and connected
tools. These agents should address a specific operational problem and run
reliably.

**The deadline**

No timeline or target launch date is provided.

## What’s Unclear

- Which business process or department should the agents support (for example,
  customer support, lead generation, or data entry)?
- Which tools, CRMs, or other software must the agents integrate with?
- What does “running smoothly” mean in measurable terms, such as response time,
  error handling, or accuracy?
- What is the project budget and expected duration?

## Three Questions to Ask Before Writing a Proposal

1. Which business process or workflow would you like to automate first?
2. Which existing tools, software, or databases—such as a CRM, email platform,
   or spreadsheets—will the agents need to use?
3. What is your target timeline for launching the initial workflow?

## What They Said vs. What They Need

**What they said:** “We need a freelancer to build and implement AI agents for
our business, design workflows, and integrate tools.”

**What they may need:** An experienced developer to identify operational
bottlenecks, define a high-impact minimum viable product (MVP), and implement a
reliable automation system that saves the team measurable time each week.

## Potential Red Flags

- **The brief lacks specifics.** Phrases such as “build and implement AI
  agents” and “integrate tools” don’t identify a concrete problem. The client
  may be interested in AI without having decided where it will provide the most
  value.
- **There is no deadline or budget.** Without these constraints, it is
  difficult to assess whether the project fits your availability, rates, and
  delivery approach.
